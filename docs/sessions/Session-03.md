# Session 03 – Writing the First Sections of the SRS

## Duration

30 Minutes

## Objective

Learn how professional software projects begin by writing the first three sections of the Software Requirements Specification (SRS): Introduction, Project Overview, and Problem Statement.

---

# Task 1 – Introduction

In this task, we wrote the introduction of the project.

### Project Name

**AI Enterprise Knowledge Assistant**

### Purpose

The application is designed to help employees quickly find answers from enterprise documents such as HR policies, SOPs, technical manuals, and company guidelines. Instead of manually searching through multiple documents, employees can ask questions in simple English and receive accurate answers with document references.

### Expected Users

* Employees
* HR Team
* Managers
* Administrators

---

# Task 2 – Project Overview

We understood the overall purpose of the product.

The AI Enterprise Knowledge Assistant is an AI-powered application that allows employees to interact with company documents using natural language. The system understands the user's question, retrieves the most relevant information, and generates a simple, easy-to-understand answer.

Unlike a traditional search system that only returns matching documents, our application provides direct answers along with references to the original documents.

---

# Task 3 – Problem Statement

We identified the real business problem before starting development.

Current challenges include:

* Employees spend too much time searching documents.
* Information is scattered across multiple files.
* New employees struggle to find company information.
* Manual searching reduces productivity.

Our solution is to build an AI-powered knowledge assistant that retrieves relevant information from enterprise documents and generates accurate answers with document references.

---

# Key Learning

Before developing software, we must clearly understand:

* What product we are building.
* Why the business needs it.
* Who will use it.
* What problem it solves.

These questions form the foundation of the entire project and guide every future design and development decision.

---

✅ Completed the **Introduction** section of the SRS.

✅ Completed the **Project Overview** section.

✅ Completed the **Problem Statement** section.

✅ Strengthened the business foundation of the project.

The project now has a clear vision, defined users, and a well-understood business problem. In the next session, we will continue the SRS by writing the **Objectives**, **Scope**, and **Stakeholders** sections, bringing us one step closer to designing and building the application.
